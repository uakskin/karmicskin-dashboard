Goal and thresholds

The goal metric and targets only decide Winners, badges and the Convert score; they are not shown on Home. Targets are the Meta-platform targets from the operator context (not the blended ROAS shown on the Shopify dashboard).

An ad must reach this lifetime spend to count as Scaled and to earn badges. Default £86 = 3× target CPA.
Ads under this spend in the report range get no Hook/Watch/Click/Convert score.

Naming convention

Ad names are split on the delimiter into segments. Segment 1 is the format, segment 2 the product. Any later part starting with V plus a number is the version, a date-looking part is the date, and everything else joins into the concept.

Access
