03 · Typography

Domine leads. Montserrat carries.

The website's pairing, adopted as the system. Georgia and bare system fonts were only ever fallbacks of convenience.

Domine · Display & headlines
Skin is a relationship to be tended.
Ancient wisdom for modern people
A warm, sturdy serif with real presence at size. Weights 400 · 500 · 600 · 700.
Montserrat · Body & interface

Montserrat carries everything Domine does not need to say out loud — body copy, labels, navigation and data. Clean and geometric, quiet enough to let the serif lead, and legible down to the smallest dashboard figure.

  • DisplayDomine · 40–68px
  • HeadlineDomine · 24–33px
  • Sub-headDomine · 18–21px
  • BodyMontserrat · 15–16px
  • Small / labelMontserrat · 11–13px
04 · Principles

How it should feel

Six principles, and the first components drawn in the proposed system.

01

Warm, never stark

Grounds are bone and cream. Cold grey is retired alongside the teal.

02

Unhurried

Generous whitespace. Nothing crowded. The brand is calm, so the layouts are too.

03

Serif leads, sans supports

Domine for what matters. Montserrat for everything that carries it.

04

Heritage with substance

Pichwai botanicals are a signature, never decoration. Earned, not borrowed.

05

Soft edges, solid buttons

12–16px radius. Confident, filled buttons. No hairline timidity.

06

Saffron is the glow

Used sparingly — a highlight, a warmth. Never a wall of yellow.

05 · The decision

The one fork that changes everything

Green-led or burgundy-led. Most of the system follows from this single call — so it is yours to make.

Recommended · v0.1

Green-led

The website's own identity, refined. Distinctly Ayurvedic — herbal, grounded, quietly premium. Burgundy stays, but as the deep seasonal accent. This is what is drawn throughout this page.

Burgundy-led

The identity your emails and landing pages already carry. Warmer and richer, closer to conventional luxury beauty. Green would step back to a supporting role. Real equity here — it is not a wrong answer.
