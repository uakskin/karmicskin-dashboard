03 · Colour in use

A colour is its job, not its hex

A palette is only as strong as its rules. Here is how each accent earns its place — what it does, and the one place it must never go.

Brick Red · #B22222

Red is action, and only action

One red action per screen. Secondary buttons stay green — filled or outline.

Red carries one message: take this action. The Add to Cart button, the checkout — the single most important tap on a page. It is never a heading, never decoration, never a text colour. Its power is scarcity: hold it back to one thing per screen and the eye goes straight to it. Bright red pops against every other colour in the palette precisely because nothing else competes with it.

Amber Honey · #EFA71D

Amber is the glow — a mark, never text

Your skin has a memory. Give it something worth remembering.

A clean, nourished canvas is the foundation of every ritual.

Bestseller   New in   Pregnancy safe

Don't

Amber as a text colour

1.8:1 contrast on Porcelain — well under the 4.5:1 text needs. It washes out.
Do

Amber behind the words

Dark Carbon ink on an amber mark — warm, and fully legible.

Amber is warmth and emphasis — the glow the brand had no colour for. It works as a mark: a highlighter wash, an underline, a badge, a fill. Dark text always sits on it or beside it. It is never the text itself. A warm highlighter swipe is also more on-brand for Karmic than coloured text would ever be — editorial, and a little hand-made.

Dark Slate Grey · #2F5350

Green carries emphasis in text

Not anti-ageing. Pro you.

When a word must carry weight, colour it green — about 7:1 contrast on Porcelain.

When a single word in a line needs to stand out, colour it green. It is fully legible on Porcelain and, because it is the brand colour, the emphasis reads as considered rather than loud. This is the answer whenever you are tempted to colour text amber — reach for green instead.

Deep Ochre · #9A6F15

Ochre is gold you can actually read

A gift for us.
The text-safe tone of the amber — for large, bold display type only.

When you want gold on the word itself — not a mark behind it — use the deep ochre. It is the amber darkened until it clears the contrast bar, so it holds up as large, bold display type. Never use it for body text; at small sizes it muddies. One hue, two tones: bright amber to mark, deep ochre to write.

Porcelain · Sand Dune · Carbon

The neutrals do the quiet work

Porcelain is every background. Sand Dune tints the occasional section so a long page has rhythm — the band this page sits a section on further down. Carbon is every word of body copy. They are calm on purpose: it is what keeps the three accents rare, and rare is what makes them land.

04 · Typography

Domine leads. Montserrat carries.

The website's pairing, adopted as the system. Georgia and bare system fonts were only ever fallbacks of convenience.

Domine · Display & headlines
Skin is a relationship to be tended.
Ancient wisdom for modern people
A warm, sturdy serif with real presence at size. Weights 400 · 500 · 600 · 700.
Montserrat · Body & interface

Montserrat carries everything Domine does not need to say out loud — body copy, labels, navigation and data. Clean and geometric, quiet enough to let the serif lead, and legible down to the smallest dashboard figure.

  • DisplayDomine · 40–68px
  • HeadlineDomine · 24–33px
  • Sub-headDomine · 18–21px
  • BodyMontserrat · 15–16px
  • Small / labelMontserrat · 11–13px
05 · Principles

Six things the system holds to

The colour and type rules above all come back to these. (And this section sits on a Sand Dune tint — the neutral doing its job.)

01

Warm, never stark

Grounds are Porcelain and Sand. Cold grey has no place here.

02

Unhurried

Generous whitespace. Nothing crowded. The brand is calm, so the layouts are too.

03

Serif leads, sans supports

Domine for what matters. Montserrat for everything that carries it.

04

Heritage with substance

Pichwai botanicals are a signature, never decoration. Earned, not borrowed.

05

One red, one job

Brick red means action, and nothing else. Reserved, so it always pops.

06

Amber glows, never speaks

Amber is a mark — an underline, a wash, a badge. Never the text itself.
