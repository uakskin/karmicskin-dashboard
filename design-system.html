01 · The brandLocked

Who Karmic Skin is, and how it speaks

Every design decision answers to this. The look exists to carry the feeling — calm, warm, and quietly sure of itself.

Skin is not a problem to be fixed. It is a relationship to be tended.

Ancient Wisdom for Modern People

What it is

A warm, pro-ageing, Ayurvedic-rooted skincare brand. Clean, concentrated formulas and ritual-led care — a calm answer to a fear-based beauty industry.

Who it is for

Women roughly 35 to 65, UK-first, many moving through perimenopause and menopause. They want comfort and steadiness, not a chase for youth.

The voice — five qualities
01

Caring & grounded

Reassuring, never childish. It wants the reader to feel looked after.

02

Human & conversational

A real person who cares — never a corporate beauty-brand team.

03

Transparent & honest

Specifics over buzzwords. Real timelines, realistic outcomes.

04

Softly poetic, always clear

Room for beauty, but the meaning never goes vague.

05

Respectful & affirming

Your skin is not the problem. It needs the right kind of care.

The mark and the wordmark

The knot logomark and the Karmic Skin wordmark. The assets exist; the usage rules below are proposed for sign-off.

Karmic Skin
Wordmark · reversed

Clear space

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the knot itself. Nothing crowds it.

Minimum size

Logomark no smaller than 24px; wordmark no smaller than 90px wide. Below that, use the mark alone.

Colour

Carbon or Dark Slate Grey on light; Porcelain on dark. Never recolour the mark to an accent.

Never

Don't stretch, rotate, add shadows or outlines, or place the mark on a busy photograph without a panel.

Note — a legacy email-signature wordmark carries the strapline "Ancient Indian Skin Science." The brand tagline is Ancient Wisdom for Modern People; the legacy asset should be retired to match.

04 · Colour in useLocked

A colour is its job, not its hex

A palette is only as strong as its rules. Here is how each accent earns its place — what it does, and the one place it must never go.

Brick Red · #B22222

Red is action, and only action

One red action per screen. Secondary buttons stay green — filled or outline.

Red carries one message: take this action. The Add to Cart button, the checkout — the single most important tap on a page. It is never a heading, never decoration, never a text colour. Its power is scarcity: hold it to one thing per screen and the eye goes straight to it.

Amber Honey · #EFA71D

Amber is the glow — a mark, never text

Your skin has a memory. Give it something worth remembering.

A clean, nourished canvas is the foundation of every ritual.

Bestseller   New in   Pregnancy safe

Don't

Amber as a text colour

1.8:1 contrast on Porcelain — well under the 4.5:1 text needs. It washes out.
Do

Amber behind the words

Dark Carbon ink on an amber mark — warm, and fully legible.

Amber is warmth and emphasis — the glow. It works as a mark: a highlighter wash, an underline, a badge, a fill. Dark text always sits on it or beside it, never in it. A warm highlighter swipe is also more on-brand for Karmic than coloured text would ever be — editorial, and a little hand-made.

Dark Slate Grey · #2F5350

Green carries emphasis in text

Not anti-ageing. Pro you.

When a word must carry weight, colour it green — about 7:1 contrast on Porcelain.

When a single word in a line needs to stand out, colour it green. It is fully legible on Porcelain and, because it is the brand colour, the emphasis reads as considered rather than loud. This is the answer whenever you are tempted to colour text amber.

Deep Ochre · #9A6F15

Ochre is gold you can actually read

A gift for us.
The text-safe tone of the amber — for large, bold display type only.

When you want gold on the word itself — not a mark behind it — use the deep ochre. It is the amber darkened until it clears the contrast bar, so it holds up as large, bold display type. Never for body text; at small sizes it muddies.

Porcelain · Sand Dune · Carbon

The neutrals do the quiet work

Porcelain is every background. Sand Dune tints the occasional section so a long page has rhythm. Carbon is every word of body copy. They are calm on purpose — it is what keeps the three accents rare, and rare is what makes them land.

05 · TypographyLocked

Domine leads. Montserrat carries.

The website's pairing, adopted as the system. A warm serif for what matters, a clean sans for everything that carries it.

Domine · Display & headlines

Montserrat · Body & interface

Montserrat carries everything Domine does not need to say out loud — body copy, labels, navigation and data. Clean and geometric, quiet enough to let the serif lead, and legible down to the smallest dashboard figure.

06 · Spacing & shapeProposed

A rhythm everything sits on

A proposed 4-point spacing scale and a small set of corner radii. One rhythm, used everywhere, is what makes a page feel unhurried rather than crowded.

Spacing scale — multiples of 4
4  xs · hairline gaps
8  sm · within a component
12  md · label to value
16  lg · default gap between cards
24  xl · card padding
32  2xl · block separation
48  3xl · section breathing room
64  4xl · top and tail of a section
Corner radius
6 · pills, tags
12 · buttons, inputs
16 · cards, panels
100 · badges
Layout

Generous, never tight

Content holds to a ~1080px column with wide gutters. White space is a feature — the brand is calm, the layouts breathe.

Soft edges, hairline borders

Surfaces are white cards on Porcelain, separated by a 1px warm line rather than heavy shadow. Lift only on hover.

08 · PrinciplesLocked

Six things the system holds to

If a decision is ever unclear, it comes back to these.

01

Warm, never stark

Grounds are Porcelain and Sand. Cold grey has no place here.

02

Unhurried

Generous whitespace. Nothing crowded. The brand is calm, so the layouts are too.

03

Serif leads, sans supports

Domine for what matters. Montserrat for everything that carries it.

04

Heritage with substance

Pichwai botanicals are a signature, never decoration. Earned, not borrowed.

05

One red, one job

Brick red means action, and nothing else. Reserved, so it always pops.

06

Amber glows, never speaks

Amber is a mark — an underline, a wash, a badge. Never the text itself.
