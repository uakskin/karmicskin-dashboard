How it works

Using this page

Editing. Click Edit plan. Drag a bar to move it, drag either end to make it longer or shorter, or pick the start and end halves from the dropdowns. Rename, change the workstream or status, or remove an activity in the same place. Every change saves on its own.

Order. Choose a new number in the # column to move an activity up or down the list. Turn on Auto-order by start date and the list re-sorts itself every time a bar moves, so the sequence always follows the timeline.

Reading it. A diamond is a milestone: a date, not a stretch of work. Finished activities turn Citron. Anything marked at risk carries an amber badge, and the amber line is today.

Starting point. The first version of this plan was seeded on 10 October 2026 from the 2026-27 Business Tracker (Goal 2 and the 13 Week Plan). Dates the tracker set are used as given; the rest are estimates to be corrected here.
