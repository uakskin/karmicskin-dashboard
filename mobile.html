How these are worked out

Shopify revenue: Shopify's own "Total sales" (from Shopify Analytics), so it matches Shopify exactly: sales after discounts and returns, including VAT and shipping. Like Shopify, an order edit, return or cancellation counts on the day it happens.

Blended ROAS: Shopify revenue ÷ Meta and Google ad spend combined. Amber under the figure means it is below the 2.20 target.

Repeat orders: orders from returning customers, as Shopify counts them, ÷ all orders.

AOV: Shopify revenue ÷ orders, so it includes VAT and shipping. Shopify's own "Average order value" leaves both out, so it reads lower.

Amazon sales: Amazon ordered product sales (same as the Amazon dashboard). Today's figure is provisional until Amazon's nightly report. All Sales = Shopify revenue + Amazon sales.

Amazon ROAS: Amazon sales ÷ Amazon ad spend (Sponsored Products, Brands and Display), worked out the same way as the Amazon dashboard: sales only count on days whose ad spend has arrived.

Shopify figures are live: the page asks Shopify every 15 seconds while it is open. Ad spend refreshes every 10 minutes (Meta sends spend every 30), Amazon sales every 30, so today's ROAS is live revenue over the latest ad spend.

The main dashboard on the computer still uses the warehouse's own count of Shopify sales, which can differ from Shopify by a few pounds a day.

Every day is a UK day. Weeks run Monday to Sunday; the financial year starts on 1 June.
