How these are worked out

Shopify revenue: total Shopify sales including VAT and shipping, net of refunds (same as the main dashboard).

Blended ROAS: Shopify revenue ÷ Meta and Google ad spend combined. Amber under the figure means it is below the 2.20 target.

Repeat orders: orders from customers who had ordered before ÷ all orders.

AOV: Shopify revenue ÷ orders.

Amazon sales: Amazon ordered product sales (same as the Amazon dashboard). Today's figure is provisional until Amazon's nightly report.

Today's Shopify revenue, orders and repeat orders are live: they come straight from the orders, which reach us within seconds, and the page checks every 15 seconds while it is open. Earlier days and ad spend refresh every 10 minutes (Meta sends spend every 30), Amazon sales every 30. So today's ROAS is live revenue over the latest ad spend.

Every day is a UK day. Weeks run Monday to Sunday; the financial year starts on 1 June. On the small trend lines, a dashed last step means that day or week is still in progress.
